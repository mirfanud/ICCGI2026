Call for Papers:

We invite researchers, academicians, and practitioners to contribute to the 4th International Conference on Cognitive and Generative Intelligence (ICCGI 2026), to be held at Abdul Wali Khan University Mardan. This biennial conference continues the legacy of the ICCIS series. It serves as a premier platform for the exchange of innovative ideas and breakthrough research in the multidisciplinary domains of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cognitive Computing, and Intelligent Systems.
 
We welcome submissions addressing novel theories, models, applications, and trends in — but not limited to — the following areas:

  • Cognitive Computing and Brain-Inspired AI
  • Generative Artificial Intelligence and Foundation Models
  • Machine Learning and Deep Learning
  • Natural Language Processing and Speech Technologies
  • Computer Vision and Image Understanding
  • Human-AI Collaboration and Interaction
  • Robotics, Automation, and Intelligent Systems
  • AI for Science, Engineering, and Education
  • Cybersecurity, Trustworthy, and Ethical AI
  • Data Science, Analytics, and Big Data Applications
  • Computational Modeling and Simulation
  • Quantum and Neuromorphic Computing
  • Optimization and Evolutionary Algorithms
  • Mathematical and Statistical Foundations of AI
  • Intelligent Decision Support and Knowledge Systems
 
Authors are encouraged to submit original research papers, review articles, and case studies that address emerging challenges and opportunities in the evolving landscape of AI. All submissions will undergo rigorous peer review, and accepted papers will be published in the conference proceedings by the international publisher Springer. Selected high-quality papers may also be considered for special journal issues.

There is a multidisciplinary track addressing all areas other than the main theme of the conference, and authors are welcome to join us. 

Submission Guidelines:

  • All papers must be original and not simultaneously submitted to or considered for publication in any other journal or conference. Research papers must belong to the various topics of interest relevant to the conference sessions.
  • All papers should be in English and follow the two-column format of the IEEE Conference style guidelines. Templates can be downloaded using the link below. Page length must not be more than 6 pages.
  • Authors are encouraged to use the LaTeX template (https://www.ieee.org/conferences/publishing/templates.html) for typesetting their manuscripts. Documents created in a word may result in unnecessary processing delay when submitting camera-ready copies. Papers that do not comply with style guidelines will not be published in IEEE Xplore.
  • Figures and plots must be of good quality. Text appearing in figures must be of the same size as the normal document text. Make sure that figures and plots appear fine and distinguishable when printed in black and white, as well as when viewed on-screen. Plots created in Excel or figures/plots taken as screenshots should be avoided unless they are in high resolution.
  • Prepared manuscripts can be submitted electronically via the system given below.
  • If there are any problems with the submission website, please contact the program organizer at [email protected].
 
Submissions should be up to 6 pages (including title and figures but excluding references) and should include the following sections only:
  • Abstract
  • Introduction (Problem statement, an overview of the proposed work, main differences from existing works)
  • Related work
  • Methodology of the Proposed Work
  • Preliminary results
  • Conclusion
  • References
 
Submissions will be processed based on the importance, clarity, excellent understanding of the core related work, and overall quality of the submission paper. The conference proceedings will be published online.
