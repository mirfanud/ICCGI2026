Workshop # 1

Postgraduate Research Evaluations and Presentations 2026
​(PREP 2026)

You are a student of MS or PhD, and as part of your degree requirements you need conference presentation without publishing the full proceeding of paper, here is your chance to present!!!
The workshop is a research-oriented workshop designed for MS and PhD students to present their ongoing work in front of academic and industry experts for constructive feedback, evaluation, and scholarly guidance. The event aims to strengthen research quality, encourage interdisciplinary discussion, and help students align their work with higher education standards, including HEC requirements. It provides a collaborative platform to refine ideas, improve research direction, and foster innovation through critical review and academic mentorship.

Objectives

  • To provide MS and PhD scholars a structured platform to present their research work and receive expert academic feedback for improvement and refinement.
  • To enhance the quality of postgraduate research by facilitating critical evaluation, interdisciplinary discussion, and alignment with HEC and international research standards.
  • To promote a culture of scholarly collaboration, innovation, and mentorship between students, faculty, and research experts across different domains.

Outcomes

  • Improved quality and clarity of MS and PhD research work through structured expert feedback and academic review.
  • Strengthened alignment of student research with HEC guidelines and internationally accepted scholarly standards.
  • Enhanced research communication, presentation skills, and interdisciplinary collaboration among postgraduate scholars and faculty members.

Workshop # 2

Poster Presentations 2026
PP2026

You are a student at BS, MS, PhD, here is your chance to present your work!!!
Poster Presentations 2026 will be a dedicated platform for undergraduate, graduate, and early-stage researchers to visually showcase their research contributions in the domains of Computational Intelligence, Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, and associated interdisciplinary areas. The session aims to encourage emerging scholars to present innovative ideas through illustrative posters, enabling knowledge exchange, feedback from experts, and inspiring a collaborative research culture.


Participants will have the opportunity to interact directly with reviewers, discuss methodologies, defend findings, and refine their research perspectives. The Best Poster award will further promote excellence and motivation among young researchers.
​

This workshop will run parallel to the main ICCGI2026 technical sessions, serving as an engaging research exhibition space where creativity meets scientific communication.

Objectives

  • To provide a platform for early-stage researchers and students to visually present innovative research ideas and findings.​
  • To promote scientific dialogue, feedback exchange, and collaborative interaction among presenters, experts, and attendees.

Outcomes

  • Enhanced research communication skills and technical presentation confidence among participants.
  • Recognition of outstanding research through awards, leading to motivation and increased participation in future scientific events.

Workshop # 3

AI Meets Imagination with a Hands-on Journey in Robotics and Code 2026
​(AMI-HJRC2026)

AI Meets Imagination with a Hands-on Journey in Robotics and Code 2026
The workshop aims to empower students and young innovators from NMDs disciplines across Pakistan with practical skills in coding, artificial intelligence, and robotics. This initiative seeks to bridge the gap between creativity and technology by providing participants with experiential learning opportunities that integrate design thinking with emerging digital tools. The workshop will focus on promoting innovation, problem-solving, and interdisciplinary collaboration, enabling NMDs participants to apply AI and robotics concepts in digital design, interactive media, and creative technology projects. Through this event, we aspire to nurture a new generation of designers and technologists capable of contributing to Pakistan’s growing digital economy and innovation ecosystem. 

Objectives

  • To build technical and creative capacity among students and young innovators, particularly from New Media Design (NMDs) and related disciplines, through hands-on training in Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, and Coding.
  • To promote interdisciplinary collaboration by connecting participants from design, computing, and engineering backgrounds, encouraging innovative applications of AI and robotics in creative media and human-centered design.
  • To enhance STEM awareness and inclusion by ensuring active participation from students of the Newly Merged Districts (NMDs) of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, supporting equitable access to emerging technology education. 
  • To position Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) as a growing hub for digital innovation, creative technology, and applied AI research through knowledge exchange, skill development, and academic–industry linkages fostered during the workshop.
  • The event aims to promote interdisciplinary collaboration, enhance research visibility, and inspire innovation in the rapidly evolving domains of intelligent computing, human-AI interaction, and data-driven knowledge systems.  
  • The event will be a venue for spreading STEM awareness and skills development in NMDs.

Outcomes

  • Participation of 150–200 researchers and students from institutions at NMDs.
  • Workshops and tutorials conducted by researchers to train young scientists.
  • Enhanced visibility of AWKUM and KP research in the international AI community.
  • Promote STEM awareness in NMDs.
