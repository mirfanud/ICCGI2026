The 4th International Conference on Cognitive and Generative Intelligence (ICCGI 2026) marks the next milestone in a successful series of international conferences on emerging trends in Artificial Intelligence and Computational Intelligence. Building upon the achievements of its predecessors, particularly the 3rd International Conference on Computational Intelligent Systems 2024 (ICCIS 2024) held at Kohat University of Science and Technology, this edition aims to expand the discourse toward the rapidly evolving domains of Cognitive and Generative Intelligence.
 
This year, ICCGI 2026 will be jointly hosted by Abdul Wali Khan University, Mardan, and University of Swabi to bring together researchers, scientists, industry professionals, and graduate scholars from around the world to explore state-of-the-art developments in AI. The conference will provide an interdisciplinary platform for presenting novel research in generative models, cognitive computing, deep learning, data analytics, and human-AI collaboration, emphasizing their transformative role in modern science, technology, and society. 
 
In continuation of the conference academic tradition, ICCGI 2026 will feature keynote talks by international experts, technical sessions, poster presentations, and panel discussions. A special focus will be placed on MS and PhD students, providing them with an opportunity to present their research, receive constructive feedback, and engage with leading scholars in the field.
 
As the landscape of AI continues to evolve from computational to generative paradigms, ICCGI 2026 aims to serve as a catalyst for new ideas, collaborations, and innovations that enhance Pakistan’s position in global AI research. The event is organized in hybrid mode at AWKUM Garden Campus, aiming to develop inclusion, with active participation from researchers across the country, particularly from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and the Newly Merged Districts.
 
Through this fourth conference in the series, AWKUM aspires to establish a sustainable international forum for advancing intelligent, ethical, and human-centric AI research in Pakistan.
 
The conference series is organized biennially to highlight emerging trends in Artificial and Computational Intelligence. The previous three events in this series of conferences on advanced topics in AI were organized with the details given below:

  • ICCIS2024 - 3rd International Conference on Computational Intelligent Systems 2024 on 2-4 December 2024, held at KUST Kohat.
  • KUSTGW2022 - 2nd KUST Graduate Workshop 2022 on 21 February 2022, held at KUST, Kohat.
  • KUSTGW2020 - 1st KUST Graduate Workshop 2020 on 25 March 2020, held at KUST, Kohat.

Call for Papers

We invite researchers, academicians, and practitioners to contribute to the 4th International Conference on Cognitive and Generative Intelligence (ICCGI 2026), to be held at Abdul Wali Khan University Mardan. This biennial conference continues the legacy of the ICCIS series and serves as a premier platform for the exchange of innovative ideas and breakthrough research in the multidisciplinary domains of Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cognitive Computing, and Intelligent Systems.

Conference Topics

Authors are encouraged to submit papers that address the theoretical foundations, methodological approaches, and practical applications of the conference's main theme: Cognitive and Generative Intelligence. Further division of topics into multiple specialized and multidisciplinary tracks are given below.

Conference Main Track
  • Cognitive Computing and Brain-Inspired AI
  • Generative Artificial Intelligence and Foundation Models
  • Machine Learning and Deep Learning
  • Natural Language Processing and Speech Technologies
  • Computer Vision and Image Understanding
  • Human-AI Collaboration and Interaction
  • Robotics, Automation, and Intelligent Systems
  • AI for Science, Engineering, and Education
  • Cybersecurity, Trustworthy, and Ethical AI
  • Data Science, Analytics, and Big Data Applications
  • Computational Modeling and Simulation
  • Quantum and Neuromorphic Computing
  • Optimization and Evolutionary Algorithms
  • Mathematical and Statistical Foundations of AI
  • Intelligent Decision Support and Knowledge Systems
Specialized Workshops
  • Hands-on Training Workshops
  • Industrial Linkages
  • Specialized Workshops for NMDs participants
  • Specialized workshop by foreign academia/industry
  • Mentorship Sessions
  • Poster Presentations
  • Brainstorming Sessions
  • VC Summit - Pathways to Progress
Authors are encouraged to submit original research papers, review articles, and case studies that address emerging challenges and opportunities in the evolving landscape of AI. All submissions will undergo rigorous peer review, and accepted papers will be published in the conference proceedings by the international publisher Springer. Selected high-quality papers may also be considered for special journal issues.
